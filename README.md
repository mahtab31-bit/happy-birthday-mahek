# 🎉 Happy Birthday Mahek! 🎂

## A Birthday Celebration From Sister to Sister Across the Miles

This is a special birthday page dedicated to my amazing sister, Mahek, celebrating her birthday even though we're thousands of miles apart – her in Canada and me in India.

### 💝 About This Page

Just like I created a special page for my husband (bemyvalentine), I wanted to create something unique and heartfelt for my sister's birthday. This page captures:

- 🎈 The joy of celebrating across the distance
- 💕 Heartfelt wishes and love
- 🌍 The bond that connects us despite the miles
- ✨ Beautiful animations and celebrations

### 🎨 Features

✨ **Animated Balloons** - Floating balloons that celebrate throughout the page

💌 **Heartfelt Message** - A personal note expressing love across the distance

🌟 **Birthday Wishes** - Six special wishes for the year ahead

💝 **Memory Section** - A touching note about our bond

🎁 **Festive Design** - Colorful gradient background and interactive elements

✨ **Confetti Effect** - Celebratory confetti that falls when the page loads

📱 **Responsive Design** - Beautiful on all devices
